How PetCare QR Identification Works

If your pet ever wanders away, reconnecting is simple, instantaneous, and secure.

1. Register & Generate Tag

Register your pet, customize owner visibility, and instantly generate an encrypted, printable collar tag and card.

2. Attach to Pet Collar

Print or attach the lightweight QR code directly to your pet's collar. It remains durable, permanent, and stable.

3. Instant Scan & Report

A finder simply scans the code with their mobile phone camera—no app needed—views authorized emergency info, and submits an alert.

Privacy & Security By Design
